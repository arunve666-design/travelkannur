Beaches of Kannur

Five stunning beaches — from famous drive-in shores to hidden fishing villages

കണ്ണൂരിലെ കടൽത്തീരങ്ങൾ

01
Most Popular

Payyambalam Beach പയ്യാമ്പലം ബീച്ച്

Kannur's most beloved city beach — a long golden stretch perfect for evening walks. The famous Payyambalam Garden sits alongside, making it ideal for families and couples.

Sunsets here are spectacular with the sky painted in brilliant oranges and pinks. Well maintained with food stalls and benches nearby.

📍 2km from Kannur city centre
🕐 Best time: 5 PM – 7 PM for sunsets
🎡 Children's park and garden nearby
🚗 Free parking available
✉️ info@travelkannur.in
02
Asia's Longest Drive-In Beach

Muzhappilangad Beach മുഴപ്പിലങ്ങാട് ബീച്ച്

Asia's longest drive-in beach — 4 kilometres of hard flat sand where you can literally drive your car or bike right along the shore. A completely unique experience in all of India.

Rocky outcrops on both ends create a natural boundary. Perfect for a sunrise drive with the sea breeze.

📍 16km from Kannur, near Thalassery
🚗 Drive-in allowed — 4km of driveable sand
🏍️ Bike & car rentals available nearby
🌅 Best at sunrise or late evening
✉️ info@travelkannur.in
03
Hidden Gem

Meenkunnu Beach മീൻകുന്ന് ബീച്ച്

Kannur's best kept secret — a quiet, unspoilt beach tucked between coconut groves and fishing villages. Perfect for escaping the crowds and experiencing an authentic Kerala beach.

Watch local fishermen bring in their daily catch every morning. Photography here is breathtaking — especially at golden hour.

📍 8km from Kannur city centre
🤫 Very peaceful, minimal tourists
📸 Excellent photography spot
🐟 Watch fishermen at work in the morning
✉️ info@travelkannur.in
04
Scenic & Serene

Ettikulam Beach എട്ടിക്കുളം ബീച്ച്

Ettikulam Beach is a beautiful, relatively undiscovered beach near Kannur known for its pristine waters and peaceful surroundings. Far less crowded than the more famous beaches.

Rocky cliffs on one side and open sea on the other make this beach uniquely picturesque — a favourite for nature lovers and photographers.

📍 North Kerala coast, near Kannur
🌊 Clean pristine waters, less crowded
🏞️ Rocky cliffs and natural scenery
🌅 Stunning sunrise and sunset views
✉️ info@travelkannur.in
05
Fishing Village Charm

Chootad Beach ചൂതാട് ബീച്ച്

Chootad Beach offers a genuine glimpse into the traditional coastal life of Kannur. This charming fishing village beach is lined with colourful boats and is far from the tourist trail.

Walk along the shore and interact with local fishermen for an unforgettable authentic cultural experience of Kerala's coast.

📍 Coastal village near Kannur
🚣 Colourful traditional fishing boats
🎣 Watch traditional fishing techniques
📸 Ideal for authentic Kerala photography
✉️ info@travelkannur.in

🌊 Beach Tips for Kannur

  • ☀️ Visit early morning (6–8 AM) or evening (5–7 PM) to avoid heat
  • 👗 Dress modestly — Kannur is a conservative coastal area
  • 🚰 Carry drinking water — stalls are limited at some beaches
  • 🌊 Check sea conditions before swimming — Arabian Sea can have strong currents
  • 🚗 Rent a two-wheeler (₹400/day) to visit multiple beaches in one day
  • 📸 Sunset shots at Payyambalam and Ettikulam are spectacular

Kannur Carnival 2026

കണ്ണൂർ കാർണിവൽ 2026 — പോലീസ് മൈതാനത്ത്

Kannur Carnival 2026 is the big city fair running from 1 May to 31 May 2026 at Police Maidan (Police Ground), Kannur — the city's main fairground right in the heart of town. A full month of amusement rides, food stalls, shopping, live entertainment and games for the whole family.

The carnival is a yearly highlight for people of Kannur and surrounding districts — a perfect evening out for families, students, couples and groups. Follow the official Instagram @kannurcarnival2026 for the latest schedule, performer line-ups and daily highlights.

🚗 How to reach Police Maidan, Kannur

Police Maidan (also called Police Ground) is right in the centre of Kannur city — walking distance from KSRTC Bus Stand and a short auto-rickshaw ride from Kannur Railway Station (~2 km). From Kannur International Airport (CNN), it is about 30 km / ~45 minutes by taxi. Visit in the evening when the fair is in full swing.

💡 Tips for visitors

  • 🕖 Best time to visit: after 6 PM — the fair really comes alive in the evening
  • 💵 Carry small cash for individual ride/game tickets and food stalls
  • 👟 Wear comfortable shoes — you'll be on your feet exploring
  • 🅿️ Parking can get crowded on weekends — consider an auto-rickshaw if you're nearby
  • 📱 Check @kannurcarnival2026 on Instagram for daily programme updates

🥭 In Season

Malabar Mangoes & Jackfruit

May is peak season for North Kerala mangoes (Naattu Maanga, Kilichundan) and jackfruit. Any roadside stall in Kannur, Thalassery or Payyanur will have fresh fruit at unbeatable prices.

🏖️ Drive-In Beach Season

Last chance before the monsoon

Muzhappilangad Drive-in Beach is at its best now — firm sand, calm sea. From June onwards the monsoon makes driving on the beach unsafe and the strip is usually closed to vehicles.

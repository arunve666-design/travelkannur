👋 Greetings & Common Phrases

സുഖമാണോ?
Sukhamaano?
"How are you?" — the universal greeting after the namaskaaram.
ഇപ്പോ വരാ
Ippo varaa
"I'll come right now" — but in Kannur this often means "in a while" 😄
Heard everywhere — don't take it literally.
എന്താ പറയണത്?
Enthaa parayanath?
"What's up?" / "What are you saying?" — casual hi.
പിന്നെ കാണാം
Pinne kaanaam
"See you later" — standard goodbye.
എനക്കറിയില്ല
Enakkariyilla
"I don't know." Note the Kannur "enakku" instead of standard "enikku".
"Enakku" vs "Enikku" is a giveaway you're in North Malabar.
ഞമ്മള്
Njammal
"We" / "us" — the very Malabar way of saying nammaḷ.
Pure North Malabar / Kannur trademark word.
ഇഞ്ഞി
Inji
"You" — informal "nee" used in Kannur, especially among friends.
Wrong context = rude. Use with close friends only.
ഞാമ്മൻ
Njaamman
"Me" / "I" — colloquial Kannur first person.
കിടു
Kidu
"Awesome" / "Top-class". Modern Malabar slang for something great.
പൊളി
Poli
"Killer" / "Bombastic". Used for anything amazing — a film, food, a goal.

👨‍👩‍👧 Family & Relations

ഉമ്മ
Umma
Mother (predominantly Muslim households in Kannur).
ബാപ്പ
Baappa
Father (predominantly Muslim usage).
അമ്മായി
Ammayi
Aunt — mother's sister or father's brother's wife.
കാക്ക
Kaakka
Elder brother — common in Muslim households of Kannur & Mappila community.
ഇത്താത്ത
Ithaattha
Elder sister — used in Muslim families across Kannur.
മാമ
Maama
Mother's brother (uncle).
വല്ലിമ്മ
Vallimma
Grandmother — affectionate, Mappila Muslim usage.
വല്ലിപ്പ
Vallippa
Grandfather — Mappila Muslim usage.
മോൻ / മോള്
Mone / Mole
Son / Daughter — also used as an affectionate "dear" to younger people.
ചേട്ടൻ
Chettan
Elder brother — also used to address any older man you don't know by name.
ചേച്ചി
Chechi
Elder sister — same usage as chettan for women.

🍛 Food & Drink

പത്തിരി
Pathiri
Thin, soft rice flatbread — the iconic Malabar breakfast bread, eaten with curry.
കല്ലുമ്മക്കായ
Kallummakkaya
Mussels — typically stuffed with rice batter and steamed (Arikkadukka).
Kannur's signature seafood. Try at any toddy shop.
അരിക്കടുക്ക
Arikkadukka
Stuffed mussels — Kannur's most famous beach snack.
കൂട്ടാൻ
Koottaan
"Curry" / accompaniment — the side dish for rice. Pure Malabar usage.
കഞ്ഞി
Kanji
Rice porridge — Kannur's comfort food, often eaten with payar (green gram) and pappadam.
പായസം
Payasam
Sweet milk pudding — served at festivals and Onam.
ഉണ്ണിയപ്പം
Unniyappam
Sweet rice-jaggery fritters — temple snack and tea-time favourite.
ഹൽവ
Halwa
Sweet jelly-like dessert. Kannur Halwa (Mannur Halwa) is famous across Kerala.
ചായ
Chaaya
Tea — usually means strong black tea with milk. "Chaaya kudi" = "have tea".
കള്ള്
Kallu
Toddy — palm wine. The toddy shops (kallu shappu) of Kannur are legendary for seafood.
പൊരിച്ചത്
Porichathu
Anything fried — "meen porichathu" = fried fish, the toddy shop classic.
മീൻ
Meen
Fish — central to every Kannur meal. Sardines (mathi), king fish (ney meen), pearl spot (karimeen) all common.
വറുത്ത്
Varuthathu
Roasted / pan-fried — applies to fish, beef, chicken.
ബിരിയാണി
Biriyani
Biryani — Thalassery Biryani is the famous Kannur version, made with khaima rice.

🏠 Daily Life & Things

വീട്
Veedu
House / home.
പറമ്പ്
Paramb
Compound / yard around the house — every Kannur home has one with coconut and areca trees.
തെങ്ങ്
Thenge
Coconut tree — everywhere in Kannur.
വെള്ളം
Vellam
Water.
പണി
Pani
Work — "Pani undo?" = "Got work?" Also used as "trouble": "Avante pani aayi" = "He's in trouble."
പൈസ
Paisa
Money / cash.
വണ്ടി
Vandi
Vehicle — car, bike, bus, anything that moves you around.
ബസ്സ്
Bus
Bus. Kannur's private buses are an experience in themselves — loud horns, fast driving, painted artwork.
കടവ്
Kadavu
Riverside / boat-landing — "Parassinikadavu" = boat landing at Parassini.
കടല്
Kadal
Sea / ocean.
പുഴ
Puzha
River. Kannur's main rivers: Valapattanam, Anjarakandi, Mahe.
മഴ
Mazha
Rain — Kannur sees heavy monsoon June–September.

💬 Slang & Expressions

ഉണ്ടല്ലോ
Undallo
"Isn't it there?" / "Yeah right" — sarcastic, depending on tone.
ഇതെന്തൂട്ടാ
Ithentootta
"What the heck is this?" — Kannur version of "ithenthaanu".
"-ootta" suffix is very North Malabar.
എന്തൂട്ടാ പറയണത്
Enthootta parayanath
"What are you even saying?" — used in mild disbelief or frustration.
വേണ്ട
Venda
"Don't need" / "No thanks" — said with a head shake.
പറ്റും
Pattum
"Will work" / "Possible" / "OK". The Kannur "yes, we can".
പറ്റൂല
Pattoola
"Can't" / "Not possible" — opposite of pattum.
ഭയങ്കരം
Bhayankaram
"Terrific" / "Massive" — can mean either great or terrible, context decides.
കത്തി
Kathi
Long boring speech / lecture. "Avan kathi vekkunnu" = "He's giving a long boring talk".
പിടി കിട്ടി
Pidi kitti
"Got it" / "Understood". Literally "caught hold of".
പറ്റിച്ചു
Pattichu
"Cheated me" / "Tricked me". "Avan ente paisa pattichu" = "He cheated me of my money".
ഉറപ്പാ
Urappaa
"For sure" / "Definitely". "Urappayi varum" = "Will definitely come".
സിമ്പിൾ
Simple
English loanword used liberally — "simple kaaryam aanu" = "it's a simple thing".

🔥 Theyyam & Culture

തെയ്യം
Theyyam
The ritual art form unique to North Malabar — a person becomes a god through dance, costume and trance. Most distinctive Kannur tradition.
കാവ്
Kaavu
Sacred grove / shrine where Theyyam is performed. Every Kannur village has at least one.
കളിയാട്ടം
Kaliyaattam
Annual Theyyam festival at a particular kaavu — usually 1–4 days.
തിറ
Thira
Variant of Theyyam, often performed in Brahmin temples in southern Kannur and Kozhikode.
തോറ്റം
Thottam
The invocation song that summons the Theyyam deity — performed before the full costume comes on.
കോലം
Kolam
The form / costume of a Theyyam. Each deity has a unique kolam.
മുടി
Mudi
The towering headgear of a Theyyam — often 20–30 feet tall in some forms.
ചെണ്ട
Chenda
The cylindrical drum that drives Theyyam, temple festivals and Kalaripayattu.
കളരി
Kalari
The traditional gymnasium where Kalaripayattu (Kerala's martial art) is practised.
കളരിപ്പയറ്റ്
Kalaripayattu
Kerala's ancient martial art — Kannur has many famous kalaris.
പൂരം
Pooram
Temple festival — featuring elephants, percussion and fireworks.
ഉത്സവം
Utsavam
Festival — generic word for any annual temple festival.
മാപ്പിളപ്പാട്ട്
Mappilapattu
Mappila song — folk music of Kerala Muslims, especially popular in Kannur & Malappuram.

📍 Places & Directions

കണ്ണൂർ
Kannur
Kannur — also historically known as Cannanore. From "Kanathur" or "Kannan-ooru" (city of Krishna).
തലശ്ശേരി
Thalassery
Thalassery (Tellicherry) — famous for biryani, cake, and circus.
പയ്യന്നൂർ
Payyanur
Payyanur — town in North Kannur famous for its Subrahmanya temple.
പറശ്ശിനിക്കടവ്
Parassinikadavu
Parassinikadavu — village famous for the Sri Muthappan temple and daily Theyyam.
വടക്ക്
Vadakku
North.
തെക്ക്
Thekku
South.
കിഴക്ക്
Kizhakku
East.
പടിഞ്ഞാറ്
Padinjaaru
West.
അങ്ങാടി
Angaadi
Bazaar / market street — every Kannur town has one as its centre.
ജംഗ്ഷൻ
Junction
Road junction — used in every Kannur village as a meeting point. "Bus stand junction-il erangu" = "Get off at the bus stand junction".
സ്റ്റോപ്പ്
Stoppu
Bus stop. "Adutha stoppil erangaam" = "Will get down at the next stop".
